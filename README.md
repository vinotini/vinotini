## 🧠 About Me

I’m Vinotini, a final-year Computer Science student with a strong inclination toward solving real-world challenges through the lens of AI, privacy, and ethical tech. My journey in tech isn’t just about building — it’s about **building responsibly**.

I specialize in:
- Designing **AI-powered systems** that are explainable and actionable  
- Creating **privacy-aware applications** that detect and mitigate sensitive data exposure  
- Leveraging **cloud platforms** like AWS and Azure for scalable, real-time solutions  

🔒 Currently working on **PhantomAuth** — a behavioral authentication system that uses typing and scrolling patterns to verify users, aiming for a future of passwordless privacy.

I approach every project with curiosity, attention to detail, and a user-first mindset. Whether it's designing a document privacy scanner, diagnosing diabetic retinopathy with deep learning, or exploring behavioral authentication, I strive to create tools that are both impactful and intentional.

I believe the best technology is the one that empowers people — not overwhelms them.
